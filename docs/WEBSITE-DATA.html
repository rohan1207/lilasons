7. Company / About

Subtitle: 30 Years and Counting

The Lilasons Group, a pioneering entrepreneurial entity since 1971, has an illustrious history rooted in the dynamic Indian beer industry, with world-renowned brands such as Kangaro and Khajuraho. With a visionary spirit that inspires constant evolution, the group has expanded its horizons into diverse fields, including Stainless Steel, Aluminium, and floriculture.

In a bold move, the Lilasons Group ventured into the burgeoning real estate market, drawing on its expertise in managing apartment buildings. Starting with the largest project in Aurangabad at the time, the group has since maintained the heights of success, erecting stunning residential and commercial buildings that embellish the city skyline.

The competitive real estate industry demands a deep understanding of the market and a steadfast commitment to delivering impeccable construction services. The Lilasons Group has consistently demonstrated its unwavering dedication to these ideals.

State of the Art Designs

Our associates of architects and designers have created spectacular designs. Make sure to check out our completed projects.

Lilasons Cares

Lilasons Charitable Trust is a non-profit trust established solely for extending a helping hand to the needy. In the field of health, Lilasons has donated fully automated Ambulances to hospitals in the region. Lilasons directors have actively taken part in development of hospitals and schools in the city. We also developed a one-kilometer long jogging track spread across an eight-acre area full of amazing flora.
